# Claim Strength

- `causal`: reserved for designs and analyses that support causal inference under their assumptions.
- `associational`: uses association, consistency, difference, correlation, or adjusted relationship language.
- `descriptive`: reports patterns, frequencies, observations, feasibility, or hypothesis-generating signals.

The paragraph contract uses the lowest applicable ceiling among linked study results and cited external claims. Mechanistic plausibility does not raise the ceiling of an observational result.
