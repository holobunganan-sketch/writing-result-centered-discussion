# 02 Result Ledger

Create one result entry per finding that may change the Discussion.

Required content:

- exact study source file and indexed locator;
- priority and importance;
- finding, direction, effect size, uncertainty, and analysis status;
- `causal_ceiling` derived from design and analysis;
- concrete questions the Discussion must answer.

Complete `study_profile` with population, exposure or intervention, comparator, outcomes, setting, and follow-up. Retrieval and comparability scoring use this profile.

Do not write an interpretive paragraph while its result source cannot be located in the study-evidence index.
