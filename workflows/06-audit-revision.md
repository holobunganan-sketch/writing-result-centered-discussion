# 06 Audit and Revision

The audit has two layers.

## Deterministic checks

- source and index freshness;
- Schema validity;
- study-result locator validity;
- claim excerpt and hash validity;
- contract and citation authorization;
- claim-to-result linkage;
- causal language;
- paragraph and journal limits;
- contract coverage and reverse outline.

## Codex semantic review

Complete every field in `semantic_audit_report.json` from the generated task. For each paragraph, verify:

- a single central claim;
- focus on the linked result;
- sentence-by-sentence function;
- the precise statement supported by each claim;
- loss caused by deleting each citation;
- absence of topic drift;
- evidence-strength boundaries;
- return to the study result.

`compile` repeats all checks. A prior passing report does not survive a source or draft change.
