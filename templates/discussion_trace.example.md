Our study found that structured counselling was associated with higher treatment uptake at 12 weeks. This direction is consistent with prospective evidence showing improved uptake after counselling that directly addressed the early protection gap [REF-001]. The comparison suggests that counselling content may matter because a concrete explanation of the immediate risk can resolve a specific decision barrier. Both studies used non-randomized designs, so the observed consistency supports an association and a plausible intervention target without establishing a causal effect. In similar service settings, the present finding supports prioritizing targeted counselling while retaining the need for controlled evaluation.
